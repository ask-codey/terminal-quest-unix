Mara's diary

Monday. Storm coming. Moved the spare matches to the cupboard
in the kitchen, where the damp can't reach them.

Tuesday. Found my old brass compass. Put it in the drawer by
the bed.

Wednesday. The gulls are nesting on the tower landing again.
One of them dropped something shiny into the nest. You'd never
see that nest unless you looked closely: some things here are
hidden.

Thursday. My lucky coin is safe where nobody walks. Not in a
room. Under one.
